# Follow-up starting points

Use the full conversation; adapt instead of mechanically repeating. A follow-up draft needs the same human sending approval as first outreach.

## No reply
Hey [name] — one thought on [their problem]: [specific useful observation, without pretending to have inspected inaccessible systems]. If this is still open, I'd be happy to discuss [small next step].

## Reply with a question
[Direct answer grounded in verified facts.]

[One necessary clarification or next step.]

## Gap or unfamiliar requirement
I haven't worked with [unverified requirement] yet. My experience with [verified related capability] seems transferable, but I'd want to check [specific uncertainty] before giving you a firm [timeline/commitment].

## Decline / opt-out
Thanks for letting me know. [Brief relevant closing, only if a response is appropriate.]

Do not use a closing message to reopen a rejected opportunity. Log an opt-out as DO NOT CONTACT.
