## Evidence policy

Facts may be used when explicitly confirmed by the owner or supported by a reviewed source recorded in these files. The current brief supports statements such as “I work with AI, agents, automation, websites and design.” It does not establish client history, seniority, production deployments, particular technologies, qualifications, metrics or commercial outcomes.

For each new fact record: fact, evidence URL or document, scope of contribution, verification date, and owner approval. Distinguish **SELF-REPORTED**, **EVIDENCE-REVIEWED**, and **OWNER-APPROVED**; a public repository alone does not prove authorship or results. Prefer exact, narrow claims. Never silently update this profile from opportunity requirements or inferred success.

Missing preferences need not block discovery. Show constraints as unknown and ask when they materially affect a specific decision or application. Never answer mandatory eligibility, identity or qualification questions by guessing.
